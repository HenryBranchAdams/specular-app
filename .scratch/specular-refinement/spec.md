# Specular refinement batch

Status: complete

## Objective

Reduce avoidable interface noise, make subtle actions intuitive, and automate organizational metadata without weakening Specular's authorship, privacy, provenance, or local-first guarantees.

## Comment clusters

- Automatic organization: browser comments 1 and 9.
- Calibration clarity and voice: browser comments 2 and 3.
- Interlocutor language: browser comment 4.
- Lifecycle chrome: browser comments 7, 10, and 11.
- Document geometry and typography: browser comments 6, 8, and 12.
- Reflection presentation: browser comments 5, 13, and 14.

## Approved product decisions

1. Automatic organization requires one clearly disclosed workspace consent before authored material is sent without an explicit Reflect action.
2. A manual document-title edit locks the title against later automatic replacement.
3. A generated title is private organizational metadata until the author explicitly confirms it for a snapshot.
4. Inferred block kinds remain organizational metadata. They may be corrected from Connections but do not control quotation or reference export semantics.
5. Source attachment remains an explicit author action.
6. Document and block lifecycle metadata remains durable, but active/resting/closed controls disappear from the writing surface.
7. Calibration dictation fills an editable ephemeral textbox and is sent only when the author chooses Respond.
8. User-authored prose and quoted source material are never altered to enforce interlocutor style rules.

## Extrapolated safeguards

- Automatic requests are debounced, bounded, unavailable offline, and cannot interrupt writing.
- Stale model responses cannot overwrite newer text, a manual title, or a corrected tag.
- Existing workspaces migrate without losing titles, kinds, lifecycle state, references, history, or dictation drafts.
- Removing visible labels does not remove accessible names, landmarks, keyboard focus, or touch targets.
- Automatic classification cannot cause source fields, blockquotes, or export changes without an explicit source attachment.
- The final release is verified in the live private Site after service-worker activation, not merely after a successful deployment command.

## Delivery order

Issues 01 through 07 may be implemented and tested independently. Issue 08 integrates their acceptance evidence and is the only issue authorized to deploy the batch.

## Out of scope

- Replacing the block-based document or reflection-margin interaction model.
- Making assistant output canonical writing.
- Voice commands or a voice-controlled editor.
- Removing Connections filters, lifecycle metadata, or block-kind metadata from the domain model.
- Live collaboration, billing gates, or a public launch.
