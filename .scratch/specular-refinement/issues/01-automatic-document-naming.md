# Automatic document naming

Type: enhancement
Status: resolved

## Current behavior

The author is presented with an editable Untitled thought field and must supply a document title manually. The title is reused by the library, snapshots, published pages, and exports.

## Desired behavior

Specular should generate a concise private document title once there is enough authored substance, without making the author perform a naming task. Before the first automatic organization request, the workspace must clearly disclose what writing will be sent and obtain one durable consent. A document becomes eligible after it contains at least fifty words of canonical user-authored writing and input has been idle for at least two seconds.

The first eligible request may generate a title. Any manual edit to the title permanently changes that document to author-managed naming, so later automatic responses cannot replace it. An automatic failure or offline state leaves the document usable and untitled without repeated errors or prompts.

A generated title is organizational metadata rather than canonical prose. When creating a snapshot, Specular must identify an unedited generated title as suggested and allow the author to edit or explicitly confirm it before publication.

## Key interfaces

- Workspace consent for automatic organization.
- Document title provenance: generated or author-managed.
- Automatic organization service and stale-response guard.
- Library, snapshot, sharing, export, persistence, and recovery flows.

## Acceptance criteria

- A document below fifty authored words does not trigger automatic naming.
- An eligible document triggers at most one initial naming request after the idle boundary and after consent.
- Declining consent performs no background inference and does not repeatedly interrupt writing.
- A generated title appears in the document and library and survives reload.
- A manual title edit prevents all later automatic responses from changing that title, including an already in-flight stale response.
- Offline or provider failure preserves writing and leaves title editing fully functional.
- Snapshot publication cannot silently present an unconfirmed generated title as author-owned output.
- Existing manually titled and untitled documents migrate without data loss.

## Out of scope

- Continuous title rewriting as the document evolves.
- Generating essay prose, summaries, subtitles, or descriptions.
- Publishing a generated title without author confirmation.

## Answer

Implemented consent-gated, debounced automatic naming with title provenance, stale-response protection, durable migration, manual-edit locking, and explicit snapshot confirmation for generated titles. Focused model, client, worker, and app tests cover the behavior.
