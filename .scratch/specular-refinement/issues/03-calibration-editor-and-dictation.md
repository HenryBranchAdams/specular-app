# Clear calibration editor with dictation

Type: enhancement
Status: resolved

## Current behavior

The correction editor is hidden behind a Not quite? disclosure that does not clearly read as interactive. Only keyboard entry is available.

## Desired behavior

Keep the calibration editor visibly expanded beneath the current reflection, with quiet explanatory text that makes its purpose clear. Add an inline microphone control that dictates into this textbox as a provisional, editable calibration draft.

Calibration dictation reuses the existing bounded transcription and interruption protections, but it remains ephemeral margin input. It must not create canonical writing, a document dictation draft, history, graph content, or snapshot content. The author can edit the transcript and nothing is sent to the interlocutor until Respond is chosen.

Only one microphone session may be active. Existing block dictation and calibration dictation must exclude one another. An interruption preserves already transcribed calibration text and clearly stops the recording state.

## Key interfaces

- Expanded calibration form.
- Target-aware dictation capture and transcription.
- Ephemeral calibration state and Respond action.
- Microphone permissions, interruption, and error presentation.

## Acceptance criteria

- The correction textbox is visible without expanding a disclosure.
- Its purpose and Respond action are understandable without relying on placeholder text.
- The microphone control has an accessible name, tooltip, keyboard focus, and touch target.
- Dictation text appears in the calibration textbox and remains editable before Respond.
- Respond sends the edited text as calibration context and clears the ephemeral draft only after the request starts successfully.
- Canceling or leaving the reflection does not insert calibration text into any block.
- Interruption preserves checkpointed text and makes it impossible to mistake capture for still recording.
- Block and calibration dictation cannot run simultaneously.
- Calibration text and audio remain excluded from history, Connections, snapshots, exports, and published pages.

## Out of scope

- Voice commands, voice chat, or spoken editor controls.
- Promoting calibration text into canonical writing automatically.
- Persisting session audio or calibration transcripts as workspace artifacts.

## Comments

## Answer

Replaced the hidden disclosure with an expanded labeled calibration field and added field-scoped dictation. Calibration remains editable, ephemeral, Respond-gated, mutually exclusive with document dictation, and visibly interrupted when capture fails.
