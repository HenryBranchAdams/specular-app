# Launch dictation as a Sites PWA beta

Voice dictation launches only in the standalone Sites/PWA workspace, with no visible usage quota and no embedded ChatGPT compatibility work in the initial release. Automated functional, accessibility, privacy, recovery, and export-boundary coverage plus live Sites verification are required; physical iPhone and Android interruption testing remains a documented beta follow-up rather than a release blocker, and premium gating is deferred until real cost and usage evidence exists.
