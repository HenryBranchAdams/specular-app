## Cross-catalog takeaways

- **Name by behavior and semantic job, not appearance.** The strongest examples are badge/chip/pill/tag, inline alert/callout/banner, and popover/menu/tooltip.
- **Use native structure before adding ARIA.** The catalog repeatedly favors `<form>`, `<label>`, `<nav>`, `<ol>`, `<details>`, `<summary>`, `<dialog>`, `<progress>`, and native inputs, with ARIA used to express current, selected, expanded, busy, and live states.
- **Focus, dismissal, and motion are part of the pattern.** Overlays require focus return and Escape behavior; temporary UI must remain available while hovered or focused; parallax, marquee, scramble, shimmer, carousel rotation, and similar motion need reduced-motion behavior.
- **Loading, empty, error, and completion are separate states.** Skeletons and spinners are temporary; empty states explain a valid absence; progress indicators distinguish known from unknown completion; toasts report non-blocking outcomes.
